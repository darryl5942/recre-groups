Set up the sheet (once)

The attendance sheet can't be shared outside SMU, so the app never touches it. Instead you add a small script to the sheet. It runs as you, inside your SMU account.

  2. In the attendance sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete what's in the editor, paste the script, and click Save (the disk icon).
  4. Close the Apps Script tab and reload the sheet. An Attendance menu appears next to Help.
  5. Choose Attendance → Go to the App import tab. The first time, Google asks you to allow the script: click Continue and choose your SMU account. If it says "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced → Go to … (unsafe); it's your own script. This makes the App import tab where you paste.

The tab needs Tele Handle in column A, a row of session dates above it, and optionally Name and Total columns. The script puts a 1 under the date for everyone in that session's group, adds people it hasn't seen before in the first empty rows, and never clears anything, so importing again is safe. When the app gets an update to the script, repeat steps 1–3.
